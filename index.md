---
title: Underworld Technical Notes
---

Methods, implementation notes, benchmarks and worked examples from the
[Underworld](https://github.com/underworldcode/underworld3) geodynamics code.

Each note is published as a living web article and, where it carries a DOI, as a
fixed archival PDF. The DOI identifies the archival publication; this site is the
current interactive rendition and may carry minor corrections, updated links and
discussion.

:::{note}
Pilot build — the twelve most recent months of articles. Earlier material is
still on the Ghost site and will be migrated once this format is settled.
:::
